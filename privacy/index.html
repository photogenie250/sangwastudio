Privacy Policy

Last updated: · Applies to gakoromedia.rw and all pages, subdomains and services listed below.

This Privacy Policy explains how Sangwa Studio ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information across the properties we operate:

  • Gakoro Media TV — our news and video site (this domain's homepage and /article/ pages)
  • Sangwa Studio — our photography, video and music production business (/studio/)
  • Ndera System — the school management portal we build and host for partner schools (/ndera-system-login/), used by school staff, students and parents under a separate agreement with each school

By using any of these sites, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the sites.

1. Information we collect

a. Information you give us directly

  • Booking requests (Sangwa Studio "Book your session" form): name, phone number, email, event date, budget and project details.
  • Quick contact messages: name, email, subject and message.
  • Article comments: the name you type and the text of your comment, plus the time posted. Comments are public and visible to anyone who visits the article — don't include information in a comment you don't want made public.
  • Ndera School System accounts: names, contact details, class/grade information, attendance, incidents, evaluations, permission requests and related school records, entered by school staff or parents. This data belongs to the partner school and is governed by that school's own arrangement with families; we act as the school's data processor/host.

b. Information collected automatically

  • Local/browser storage (not a server-side cookie, but similar in effect): used to remember whether you've liked an article, to save your name for the comment form, and to remember your cookie-consent choice, so we don't ask again on every visit.
  • Standard web logs from our hosting provider (e.g. approximate location from IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring site), used only in aggregate for security and to understand traffic.
  • Advertising & analytics cookies — see "Cookies & Advertising" below.

2. Cookies & advertising

Gakoro Media TV is a free news service supported in part by advertising. Where advertising is enabled on this site, we and our advertising partners (including Google AdSense) may use cookies or similar technology to:

  • show ads based on your visit to this and other sites, and
  • measure how many people see or click an ad.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or generally at aboutads.info/choices.

We may also use privacy-friendly analytics to understand overall traffic patterns (e.g. how many people read an article). This does not identify you personally.

You can control or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings at any time. Blocking them may make parts of the site (like the "Like" button remembering your choice) not work as smoothly, but the site will still function.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to bookings and messages, and to deliver the service you asked for.
  • To publish and moderate comments on articles.
  • To operate, secure and improve the sites.
  • To show relevant advertising that helps keep our news reporting free to read (see Section 2).
  • For the Ndera System, to help the school run attendance, evaluations, incident tracking and parent communication for its own students.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where information is stored

Form submissions, comments, likes and Ndera School System records are stored using Supabase, a third-party database provider, protected by row-level security so that, for example, the public can submit a booking or a comment but cannot read other people's submissions. Booking and quick-message notifications are also relayed to our own Telegram account via a Supabase Edge Function and the Telegram Bot API, for the sole purpose of alerting us to a new lead — no marketing messages are sent to you through this channel.

5. Children's privacy

The public Gakoro Media TV and Sangwa Studio sites are general-audience sites and are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through comments, bookings or messages on those sites. No advertising is shown inside the Ndera School System portal. Any student information handled inside Ndera is provided by school staff or parents under the school's own policies, used solely to operate the school's records for its own enrolled students, and is not used for advertising or shared with ad networks. Parents or guardians with questions about a specific school's records should contact that school directly, or reach us using the details below.

6. Your choices & rights

  • You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (e.g. remove a comment or a booking record), subject to what we're required to keep for legitimate business or legal reasons.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising using the Google links in Section 2.
  • You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove your "liked" status and saved comment name.

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 8.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, when we enable advertising, add analytics, or change a feature described above. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version. Continued use of the sites after a change means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact us
