About Us

News that inspires, informs and transforms.

Gakoro Media TV is Rwanda's home for credible news, inspiring stories and reporting that helps our community make sense of the world around it — on air, on our live stream, and in writing on this site.

What we do

We publish daily coverage across education, sports, community life, business, interviews and opinion, alongside a continuous live video stream. Every article on this site goes through an editorial review before it's published, and our "breaking news" ticker is reserved strictly for stories our newsroom has verified — never syndicated filler.

Who's behind it

Gakoro Media TV is produced and engineered by Sangwa Studio, a Musanze-based photo, video and music production house. Sangwa Studio also builds and hosts the Ndera School System, a records platform used by partner schools.
