About Us

We are SANGWA STUDIO

Based in MUSANZE, SANGWA STUDIO is a creative house built around one idea: every story deserves to be captured properly, whatever form it takes. That's why we work across three divisions — SANGWA PHOTO, SANGWA VIDEO and SANGWA MUSIC — sharing one crew, one standard, and one home.

Whether it's a Wedding, a Brand campaign, a Live stream or a Record you've been sitting on for months, we bring the same care to every frame and every take.

Our in-house production work extends beyond client projects: Gakoro Media is our own YouTube channel, produced and run by the SANGWA STUDIO team, showcasing original films, live coverage and behind-the-scenes content that reflect the same standard of craft we bring to every booking.
